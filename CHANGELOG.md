# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Installs from Packagist on its own: verification client, widget template and translations are
  part of the addon (no extra package needed).
- Settings page and messages in English, German, French and Spanish.

## 1.1.0

- Settings page in the Control Panel (Statamic 6): site key, protected forms, form type per form,
  behaviour during outages, widget options. The secret key stays in `.env`.

## 1.0.0

- First release: `{{ captchacore }}` tag and verification of every Statamic form submission.
